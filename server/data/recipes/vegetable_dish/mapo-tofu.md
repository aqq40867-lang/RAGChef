# Mapo Tofu

## Ingredients
Soft tofu, ground pork, doubanjiang (chili bean paste), Sichuan peppercorns, garlic, ginger, scallion, cornstarch.

## Steps
1. Cut the tofu into cubes and blanch briefly in hot water.
2. Stir-fry ground pork until browned, then add doubanjiang.
3. Add garlic, ginger, and stock, then gently add the tofu.
4. Simmer, thicken with a cornstarch slurry, and top with ground Sichuan peppercorns.
