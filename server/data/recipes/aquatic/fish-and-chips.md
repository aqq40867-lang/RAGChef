# Fish and Chips

## Ingredients
White fish fillets (cod or haddock), flour, baking powder, cold beer or sparkling water, potatoes, oil for frying, salt, malt vinegar.

## Steps
1. Cut potatoes into thick chips and fry once at a low temperature until soft.
2. Whisk flour, baking powder, and cold beer into a light batter.
3. Dip the fish in batter and deep-fry until golden and crisp.
4. Fry the chips a second time at a higher temperature until crisp, then serve with salt and malt vinegar.
