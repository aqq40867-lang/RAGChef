# Steamed Fish with Ginger and Scallion

## Ingredients
Whole white fish, ginger, scallion, soy sauce, oil.

## Steps
1. Score the fish and place ginger slices inside and on top.
2. Steam over high heat until just cooked through.
3. Discard the steaming liquid, top with fresh scallion and ginger.
4. Pour hot oil over the fish, then drizzle with soy sauce.
