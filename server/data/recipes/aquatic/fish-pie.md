# Fish Pie

## Ingredients
White fish, smoked fish, prawns, milk, butter, flour, potatoes, cheese.

## Steps
1. Poach the fish gently in milk, then flake into chunks.
2. Make a white sauce with butter, flour, and the poaching milk.
3. Combine the fish and sauce in a dish, then top with mashed potato.
4. Sprinkle with cheese and bake until golden and bubbling.
