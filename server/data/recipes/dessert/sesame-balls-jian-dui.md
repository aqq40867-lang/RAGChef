# Sesame Balls (Jian Dui)

## Ingredients
Glutinous rice flour, sugar, red bean paste, sesame seeds, oil for frying.

## Steps
1. Make a dough with glutinous rice flour, sugar, and water.
2. Wrap small portions of dough around red bean paste filling.
3. Roll the balls in sesame seeds to coat evenly.
4. Deep-fry over low heat, turning gently, until golden and puffed.
