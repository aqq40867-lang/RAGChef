# Spotted Dick

## Ingredients
Flour, suet, sugar, currants, milk, baking powder.

## Steps
1. Mix flour, suet, sugar, baking powder, and currants.
2. Bind the mixture with milk into a soft dough.
3. Shape into a log and wrap in greaseproof paper and foil.
4. Steam for a couple of hours until firm, then serve sliced with custard.
