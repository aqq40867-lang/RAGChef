# Scones with Clotted Cream and Jam

## Ingredients
Flour, baking powder, butter, sugar, milk, clotted cream, strawberry jam.

## Steps
1. Rub butter into flour, baking powder, and sugar until it resembles breadcrumbs.
2. Bind with milk into a soft dough and pat out gently.
3. Cut into rounds and bake until well risen and golden.
4. Split while warm and serve with clotted cream and jam.
