# Bakewell Tart

## Ingredients
Shortcrust pastry, raspberry jam, butter, sugar, eggs, ground almonds, flaked almonds.

## Steps
1. Line a tart tin with shortcrust pastry and spread a layer of jam over the base.
2. Cream butter and sugar, then beat in eggs and ground almonds to make the frangipane.
3. Spread the frangipane over the jam.
4. Scatter with flaked almonds and bake until golden and set.
