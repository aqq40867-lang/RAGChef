# Haggis, Neeps and Tatties

## Ingredients
Haggis, swede (neeps), potatoes (tatties), butter, black pepper.

## Steps
1. Cook the haggis according to the packet instructions, usually by simmering or baking.
2. Boil the swede and potatoes separately until tender.
3. Mash the swede and the potatoes each with butter and black pepper.
4. Serve the haggis with a mound of mashed neeps and tatties on the side.
