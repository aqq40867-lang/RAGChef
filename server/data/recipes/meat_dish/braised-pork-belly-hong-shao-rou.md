# Braised Pork Belly (Hong Shao Rou)

## Ingredients
Pork belly, rock sugar, soy sauce, star anise, ginger, scallion, Shaoxing wine.

## Steps
1. Blanch the pork belly and cut into chunks.
2. Caramelize rock sugar in oil until amber.
3. Add the pork and coat in the caramel, then add soy sauce, wine, and aromatics.
4. Simmer slowly until the pork is tender and glossy.
