# Sweet and Sour Pork

## Ingredients
Pork tenderloin, cornstarch, eggs, ketchup, sugar, vinegar, bell pepper, pineapple.

## Steps
1. Coat pork strips in egg and cornstarch, then deep-fry until crispy.
2. Mix ketchup, sugar, and vinegar into a sauce.
3. Stir-fry bell pepper and pineapple briefly.
4. Toss the fried pork in the sauce with the vegetables until coated.
