# Shepherd's Pie

## Ingredients
Minced lamb, onions, carrots, peas, tomato puree, beef stock, potatoes, butter, milk.

## Steps
1. Brown the minced lamb with onions and carrots.
2. Stir in tomato puree and stock, then simmer until thickened.
3. Mash the potatoes with butter and milk.
4. Spread the mince in a dish, top with mashed potato, and bake until golden.
