# Kung Pao Chicken

## Ingredients
Chicken breast, peanuts, dried chili peppers, scallion, ginger, garlic, soy sauce, vinegar, sugar.

## Steps
1. Dice the chicken breast and marinate it briefly.
2. Heat oil and stir-fry the dried chilies until fragrant.
3. Add the chicken and stir-fry until it changes color.
4. Add the sauce mixture and peanuts, then toss until glazed.
