# Cottage Pie

## Ingredients
Minced beef, onions, carrots, Worcestershire sauce, beef stock, potatoes, butter, milk.

## Steps
1. Brown the minced beef with onions and carrots.
2. Add Worcestershire sauce and stock, then simmer until thickened.
3. Mash the potatoes with butter and milk.
4. Top the mince with mashed potato and bake until golden on top.
