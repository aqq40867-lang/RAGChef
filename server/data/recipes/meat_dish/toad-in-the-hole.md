# Toad in the Hole

## Ingredients
Pork sausages, flour, eggs, milk, oil, onion gravy.

## Steps
1. Brown the sausages briefly in a hot oiled dish.
2. Whisk flour, eggs, and milk into a smooth batter.
3. Pour the batter around the sausages and bake until well risen and golden.
4. Serve hot with onion gravy.
