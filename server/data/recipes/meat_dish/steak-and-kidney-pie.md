# Steak and Kidney Pie

## Ingredients
Braising steak, ox kidney, onions, beef stock, flour, shortcrust or puff pastry, egg wash.

## Steps
1. Brown the steak and kidney with onions.
2. Add flour and stock, then simmer slowly until the meat is tender.
3. Transfer the filling to a pie dish and top with pastry.
4. Brush with egg wash and bake until the pastry is golden.
