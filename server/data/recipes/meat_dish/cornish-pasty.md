# Cornish Pasty

## Ingredients
Shortcrust pastry, beef skirt steak, potatoes, swede, onion, salt, pepper.

## Steps
1. Dice the beef, potatoes, swede, and onion, then season well.
2. Place the filling on a circle of pastry.
3. Fold the pastry over and crimp the edge to seal.
4. Bake until the pastry is golden and the filling is cooked through.
