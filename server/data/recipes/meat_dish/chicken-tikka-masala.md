# Chicken Tikka Masala

## Ingredients
Chicken breast, yogurt, garam masala, ginger, garlic, tomato puree, cream, chili powder.

## Steps
1. Marinate the chicken in yogurt and spices, then grill or bake until charred.
2. Fry ginger, garlic, and spices, then add tomato puree and simmer into a sauce.
3. Stir in cream and simmer until the sauce thickens.
4. Add the cooked chicken to the sauce and simmer until heated through.
