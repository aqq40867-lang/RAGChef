# Bangers and Mash

## Ingredients
Pork sausages, potatoes, butter, milk, onions, beef stock, flour.

## Steps
1. Grill or fry the sausages until browned and cooked through.
2. Boil the potatoes until tender, then mash with butter and milk.
3. Slowly caramelize sliced onions, then stir in flour and stock to make onion gravy.
4. Serve the sausages on the mash, topped with onion gravy.
