# Beef Wellington

## Ingredients
Beef fillet, mushrooms, shallots, Parma ham, Dijon mustard, puff pastry, egg wash.

## Steps
1. Sear the beef fillet quickly on all sides, then brush with mustard.
2. Finely chop mushrooms and shallots and cook down into a dry paste (duxelles).
3. Wrap the beef in Parma ham and duxelles, then encase in puff pastry.
4. Brush with egg wash and bake until the pastry is golden and the beef is cooked to preference.
