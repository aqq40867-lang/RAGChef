# Egg Drop Soup

## Ingredients
Chicken stock, eggs, cornstarch, scallion, sesame oil.

## Steps
1. Bring the stock to a gentle simmer.
2. Thicken slightly with a cornstarch slurry.
3. Slowly drizzle in beaten egg while stirring to form ribbons.
4. Finish with scallion and a few drops of sesame oil.
