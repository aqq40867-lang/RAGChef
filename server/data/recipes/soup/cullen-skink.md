# Cullen Skink

## Ingredients
Smoked haddock, potatoes, onion, milk, butter, parsley.

## Steps
1. Poach the smoked haddock in milk, then flake, reserving the milk.
2. Soften the onion in butter, then add diced potatoes and the reserved milk.
3. Simmer until the potatoes are tender.
4. Stir in the flaked haddock and parsley, and warm through before serving.
