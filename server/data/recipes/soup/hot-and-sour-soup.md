# Hot and Sour Soup

## Ingredients
Tofu, wood ear mushrooms, bamboo shoots, egg, white pepper, vinegar, cornstarch.

## Steps
1. Slice tofu, mushrooms, and bamboo shoots into thin strips.
2. Simmer everything in stock until tender.
3. Thicken with a cornstarch slurry, then drizzle in beaten egg.
4. Season with vinegar and white pepper before serving.
