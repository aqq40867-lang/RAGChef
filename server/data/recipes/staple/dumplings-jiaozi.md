# Dumplings (Jiaozi)

## Ingredients
Dumpling wrappers, ground pork, napa cabbage, ginger, scallion, soy sauce, sesame oil.

## Steps
1. Finely chop the cabbage, salt it, and squeeze out excess water.
2. Mix cabbage with ground pork, ginger, scallion, and seasonings.
3. Wrap the filling in the dumpling wrappers and seal the edges.
4. Boil, steam, or pan-fry until cooked through.
