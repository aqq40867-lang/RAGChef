# Fried Rice Noodles (Chow Fun)

## Ingredients
Flat rice noodles, beef, bean sprouts, scallion, soy sauce, dark soy sauce.

## Steps
1. Marinate and quickly stir-fry the beef, then set aside.
2. Stir-fry the rice noodles over high heat with dark soy sauce.
3. Add bean sprouts and scallion, tossing gently to avoid breaking the noodles.
4. Return the beef to the pan and combine.
