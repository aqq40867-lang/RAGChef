# Full English Breakfast

## Ingredients
Bacon, sausages, eggs, baked beans, grilled tomatoes, mushrooms, toast, black pudding.

## Steps
1. Grill the bacon, sausages, and black pudding until cooked.
2. Fry or grill the tomatoes and mushrooms.
3. Warm the baked beans and fry or poach the eggs.
4. Arrange everything on a plate with buttered toast.
