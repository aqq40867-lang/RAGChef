# Kung Pao Chicken

## Ingredients
Chicken breast, peanuts, dried chili peppers, scallion, ginger, garlic, soy sauce, vinegar, sugar.

## Steps
1. Dice the chicken breast and marinate it briefly.
2. Heat oil and stir-fry the dried chilies until fragrant.
3. Add the chicken and stir-fry until it changes color.
4. Add the sauce mixture and peanuts, then toss until glazed.

# Tomato and Egg Stir-Fry

## Ingredients
Tomatoes, eggs, salt, oil, a pinch of sugar.

## Steps
1. Cut the tomatoes into wedges.
2. Beat the eggs and season lightly with salt.
3. Scramble the eggs first and set aside.
4. Stir-fry the tomatoes until soft, then combine with the eggs.

# Mapo Tofu

## Ingredients
Soft tofu, ground pork, doubanjiang (chili bean paste), Sichuan peppercorns, garlic, ginger, scallion, cornstarch.

## Steps
1. Cut the tofu into cubes and blanch briefly in hot water.
2. Stir-fry ground pork until browned, then add doubanjiang.
3. Add garlic, ginger, and stock, then gently add the tofu.
4. Simmer, thicken with a cornstarch slurry, and top with ground Sichuan peppercorns.

# Sweet and Sour Pork

## Ingredients
Pork tenderloin, cornstarch, eggs, ketchup, sugar, vinegar, bell pepper, pineapple.

## Steps
1. Coat pork strips in egg and cornstarch, then deep-fry until crispy.
2. Mix ketchup, sugar, and vinegar into a sauce.
3. Stir-fry bell pepper and pineapple briefly.
4. Toss the fried pork in the sauce with the vegetables until coated.

# Beef and Broccoli

## Ingredients
Flank steak, broccoli, garlic, soy sauce, oyster sauce, cornstarch.

## Steps
1. Slice the beef thinly and marinate with soy sauce and cornstarch.
2. Blanch the broccoli until bright green.
3. Sear the beef quickly over high heat.
4. Combine beef and broccoli with the sauce and toss to coat.

# Vegetable Fried Rice

## Ingredients
Cooked rice (day-old), eggs, carrots, peas, scallion, soy sauce.

## Steps
1. Scramble the eggs and set aside.
2. Stir-fry diced carrots and peas until tender.
3. Add the rice, breaking up clumps, and stir-fry until heated through.
4. Mix in the eggs, soy sauce, and scallion.

# Garlic Green Beans

## Ingredients
Green beans, garlic, dried chili (optional), oil, salt.

## Steps
1. Trim the green beans and blanch or dry-fry until slightly blistered.
2. Add minced garlic and chili, stir-fry until fragrant.
3. Season with salt and toss to combine.

# Hot and Sour Soup

## Ingredients
Tofu, wood ear mushrooms, bamboo shoots, egg, white pepper, vinegar, cornstarch.

## Steps
1. Slice tofu, mushrooms, and bamboo shoots into thin strips.
2. Simmer everything in stock until tender.
3. Thicken with a cornstarch slurry, then drizzle in beaten egg.
4. Season with vinegar and white pepper before serving.

# Egg Drop Soup

## Ingredients
Chicken stock, eggs, cornstarch, scallion, sesame oil.

## Steps
1. Bring the stock to a gentle simmer.
2. Thicken slightly with a cornstarch slurry.
3. Slowly drizzle in beaten egg while stirring to form ribbons.
4. Finish with scallion and a few drops of sesame oil.

# Spicy Cold Noodles

## Ingredients
Wheat noodles, cucumber, chili oil, garlic, soy sauce, vinegar, sesame paste.

## Steps
1. Cook the noodles and rinse under cold water.
2. Julienne the cucumber.
3. Mix chili oil, garlic, soy sauce, vinegar, and sesame paste into a sauce.
4. Toss the noodles with the sauce and cucumber.

# Braised Pork Belly (Hong Shao Rou)

## Ingredients
Pork belly, rock sugar, soy sauce, star anise, ginger, scallion, Shaoxing wine.

## Steps
1. Blanch the pork belly and cut into chunks.
2. Caramelize rock sugar in oil until amber.
3. Add the pork and coat in the caramel, then add soy sauce, wine, and aromatics.
4. Simmer slowly until the pork is tender and glossy.

# Steamed Fish with Ginger and Scallion

## Ingredients
Whole white fish, ginger, scallion, soy sauce, oil.

## Steps
1. Score the fish and place ginger slices inside and on top.
2. Steam over high heat until just cooked through.
3. Discard the steaming liquid, top with fresh scallion and ginger.
4. Pour hot oil over the fish, then drizzle with soy sauce.

# Dumplings (Jiaozi)

## Ingredients
Dumpling wrappers, ground pork, napa cabbage, ginger, scallion, soy sauce, sesame oil.

## Steps
1. Finely chop the cabbage, salt it, and squeeze out excess water.
2. Mix cabbage with ground pork, ginger, scallion, and seasonings.
3. Wrap the filling in the dumpling wrappers and seal the edges.
4. Boil, steam, or pan-fry until cooked through.

# Fried Rice Noodles (Chow Fun)

## Ingredients
Flat rice noodles, beef, bean sprouts, scallion, soy sauce, dark soy sauce.

## Steps
1. Marinate and quickly stir-fry the beef, then set aside.
2. Stir-fry the rice noodles over high heat with dark soy sauce.
3. Add bean sprouts and scallion, tossing gently to avoid breaking the noodles.
4. Return the beef to the pan and combine.

# Congee (Rice Porridge)

## Ingredients
Rice, water or stock, optional toppings (century egg, shredded chicken, scallion).

## Steps
1. Rinse the rice and combine with plenty of water or stock.
2. Bring to a boil, then simmer gently, stirring occasionally.
3. Cook until the rice breaks down into a creamy porridge.
4. Serve with your choice of toppings.

# Stir-Fried Cabbage

## Ingredients
Napa or green cabbage, garlic, dried chili, vinegar, salt.

## Steps
1. Tear or slice the cabbage into bite-sized pieces.
2. Stir-fry garlic and dried chili in hot oil until fragrant.
3. Add the cabbage and stir-fry over high heat until slightly wilted.
4. Season with salt and a splash of vinegar.

# Scallion Pancakes

## Ingredients
Flour, boiling water, scallion, salt, oil.

## Steps
1. Make a dough with flour and boiling water, then let it rest.
2. Roll out the dough, brush with oil, and sprinkle with salt and scallion.
3. Roll and coil the dough, then flatten into a pancake.
4. Pan-fry until golden and crispy on both sides.

# Sesame Balls (Jian Dui)

## Ingredients
Glutinous rice flour, sugar, red bean paste, sesame seeds, oil for frying.

## Steps
1. Make a dough with glutinous rice flour, sugar, and water.
2. Wrap small portions of dough around red bean paste filling.
3. Roll the balls in sesame seeds to coat evenly.
4. Deep-fry over low heat, turning gently, until golden and puffed.

# Red Bean Soup (Sweet Dessert Soup)

## Ingredients
Red beans, rock sugar, water, dried tangerine peel (optional).

## Steps
1. Soak the red beans for a few hours or overnight.
2. Simmer the beans in water until soft.
3. Add rock sugar and tangerine peel, then simmer further.
4. Serve warm as a sweet dessert soup.

# Mango Pudding

## Ingredients
Ripe mangoes, gelatin, sugar, milk or evaporated milk, water.

## Steps
1. Blend mango flesh with sugar and a little water into a puree.
2. Dissolve gelatin in warm water and mix into the puree.
3. Stir in milk, then pour into molds.
4. Chill until set before serving.

# Fish and Chips

## Ingredients
White fish fillets (cod or haddock), flour, baking powder, cold beer or sparkling water, potatoes, oil for frying, salt, malt vinegar.

## Steps
1. Cut potatoes into thick chips and fry once at a low temperature until soft.
2. Whisk flour, baking powder, and cold beer into a light batter.
3. Dip the fish in batter and deep-fry until golden and crisp.
4. Fry the chips a second time at a higher temperature until crisp, then serve with salt and malt vinegar.

# Sunday Roast Beef with Yorkshire Pudding

## Ingredients
Beef joint, potatoes, carrots, flour, eggs, milk, beef dripping, gravy.

## Steps
1. Season the beef and roast in the oven until cooked to preference, then rest.
2. Roast potatoes and carrots alongside the beef until golden.
3. Whisk flour, eggs, and milk into a batter and bake in hot dripping until puffed for the Yorkshire puddings.
4. Carve the beef and serve with the vegetables, Yorkshire puddings, and gravy.

# Bangers and Mash

## Ingredients
Pork sausages, potatoes, butter, milk, onions, beef stock, flour.

## Steps
1. Grill or fry the sausages until browned and cooked through.
2. Boil the potatoes until tender, then mash with butter and milk.
3. Slowly caramelize sliced onions, then stir in flour and stock to make onion gravy.
4. Serve the sausages on the mash, topped with onion gravy.

# Beef Wellington

## Ingredients
Beef fillet, mushrooms, shallots, Parma ham, Dijon mustard, puff pastry, egg wash.

## Steps
1. Sear the beef fillet quickly on all sides, then brush with mustard.
2. Finely chop mushrooms and shallots and cook down into a dry paste (duxelles).
3. Wrap the beef in Parma ham and duxelles, then encase in puff pastry.
4. Brush with egg wash and bake until the pastry is golden and the beef is cooked to preference.

## Steps
1. Brown the steak and kidney with onions.

# Chicken Tikka Masala

Chicken breast, yogurt, garam masala, ginger, garlic, tomato puree, cream, chili powder.

## Steps
1. Marinate the chicken in yogurt and spices, then grill or bake until charred.

## Ingredients
Eggs, sausage meat, flour, egg wash, breadcrumbs, oil for frying.

## Steps

2. Wrap each egg in a layer of sausage meat.

# Welsh Rarebit

## Ingredients
Cheddar cheese, butter, flour, milk or beer, mustard powder, Worcestershire sauce, toast.

## Steps
1. Melt butter and stir in flour to make a roux.
2. Add milk or beer and cheese, stirring until smooth.
3. Season with mustard powder and Worcestershire sauce.
4. Spread over toast and grill until bubbling and golden.

# Lancashire Hotpot

## Ingredients
Lamb chops or shoulder, onions, potatoes, lamb stock, butter.

## Steps
1. Layer sliced lamb and onions in a casserole dish.
2. Top with overlapping slices of potato.
3. Pour in stock until nearly covering the potatoes, then dot with butter.
4. Cover and bake slowly until the meat is tender and the potatoes golden.

# Cumberland Sausage Casserole

## Ingredients
Cumberland sausages, onions, carrots, canned tomatoes, beef stock, herbs.

## Steps
1. Brown the sausages, then set aside.
2. Soften onions and carrots in the same pan.
3. Add tomatoes, stock, and herbs, then return the sausages to the pan.
4. Simmer until the sausages are cooked through and the sauce has thickened.

# Cullen Skink

## Ingredients
Smoked haddock, potatoes, onion, milk, butter, parsley.

## Steps
1. Poach the smoked haddock in milk, then flake, reserving the milk.
2. Soften the onion in butter, then add diced potatoes and the reserved milk.
3. Simmer until the potatoes are tender.
4. Stir in the flaked haddock and parsley, and warm through before serving.

# Haggis, Neeps and Tatties

## Ingredients
Haggis, swede (neeps), potatoes (tatties), butter, black pepper.

## Steps
1. Cook the haggis according to the packet instructions, usually by simmering or baking.
2. Boil the swede and potatoes separately until tender.
3. Mash the swede and the potatoes each with butter and black pepper.
4. Serve the haggis with a mound of mashed neeps and tatties on the side.

# Sticky Toffee Pudding

## Ingredients
Dates, flour, butter, sugar, eggs, bicarbonate of soda, cream, golden syrup.

## Steps
1. Simmer chopped dates with bicarbonate of soda and water until soft.
2. Beat butter and sugar, then mix in eggs, flour, and the date mixture.
3. Bake the sponge until well risen and set.
4. Make a toffee sauce with butter, sugar, and cream, and pour over the warm sponge.

# Spotted Dick

## Ingredients
Flour, suet, sugar, currants, milk, baking powder.

## Steps
1. Mix flour, suet, sugar, baking powder, and currants.
2. Bind the mixture with milk into a soft dough.
3. Shape into a log and wrap in greaseproof paper and foil.
4. Steam for a couple of hours until firm, then serve sliced with custard.

# Eton Mess

## Ingredients
Strawberries, meringue, double cream, sugar.

## Steps
1. Whip the cream until soft peaks form.
2. Hull and roughly chop the strawberries, macerating with a little sugar.
3. Crush the meringue into rough pieces.
4. Gently fold the cream, strawberries, and meringue together and serve immediately.

# Victoria Sponge Cake

## Ingredients
Butter, sugar, eggs, self-raising flour, strawberry jam, whipped cream, icing sugar.

## Steps
1. Cream the butter and sugar until light and fluffy, then beat in the eggs.
2. Fold in the self-raising flour and divide between two cake tins.
3. Bake until golden and springy, then cool completely.
4. Sandwich the sponges with jam and cream, and dust with icing sugar.

# Trifle

## Ingredients
Sponge cake, sherry, fruit or jelly, custard, whipped cream, flaked almonds.

## Steps
1. Layer sponge cake soaked in sherry at the bottom of a bowl.
2. Add a layer of fruit or set jelly on top.
3. Pour over a layer of custard and chill until set.
4. Top with whipped cream and flaked almonds before serving.

# Bread and Butter Pudding

## Ingredients
Sliced bread, butter, raisins, eggs, milk, cream, sugar, nutmeg.

## Steps
1. Butter the bread slices and layer in a dish with raisins scattered between.
2. Whisk eggs, milk, cream, and sugar into a custard.
3. Pour the custard over the bread and let it soak in.
4. Bake until set and golden on top, with a sprinkle of nutmeg.

# Treacle Tart

## Ingredients
Shortcrust pastry, golden syrup, breadcrumbs, lemon zest and juice, cream.

## Steps
1. Line a tart tin with shortcrust pastry.
2. Warm the golden syrup and stir in breadcrumbs, lemon zest, and juice.
3. Pour the filling into the pastry case.

# Jam Roly-Poly

## Ingredients
Flour, suet, baking powder, milk, raspberry or strawberry jam.

## Steps
1. Mix flour, suet, and baking powder, then bind with milk into a soft dough.
2. Roll the dough into a rectangle and spread with jam.
3. Roll up into a log shape and wrap in greaseproof paper and foil.
4. Steam or bake until firm, then slice and serve with custard.

# Bakewell Tart

## Ingredients
Shortcrust pastry, raspberry jam, butter, sugar, eggs, ground almonds, flaked almonds.

## Steps
1. Line a tart tin with shortcrust pastry and spread a layer of jam over the base.
2. Cream butter and sugar, then beat in eggs and ground almonds to make the frangipane.
3. Spread the frangipane over the jam.
4. Scatter with flaked almonds and bake until golden and set.

# Scones with Clotted Cream and Jam

## Ingredients
Flour, baking powder, butter, sugar, milk, clotted cream, strawberry jam.

## Steps
1. Rub butter into flour, baking powder, and sugar until it resembles breadcrumbs.
2. Bind with milk into a soft dough and pat out gently.
3. Cut into rounds and bake until well risen and golden.
4. Split while warm and serve with clotted cream and jam.
